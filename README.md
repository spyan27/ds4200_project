# **OpenRx: Making Medicare Patient-Administered Drug Pricing More Transparent (2019–2023)**

## **Overview**
**OpenRx** explores how the prices of patient-administered drugs covered under **Medicare Part D** changed between **2019 and 2023**. Medicare Part D provides prescription drug coverage for Medicare enrollees—since **Original Medicare (Parts A & B)** does *not* cover retail prescription medications, Part D fills that gap.

This project includes three main components:

1. **General Pricing Trends (2019–2023):**  
   Examining broad price changes over the five-year period.

2. **Manufacturer-Level Analysis:**  
   Highlighting how pricing patterns differ across manufacturers, including notable increases and outliers.

3. **Disease-Focused Price Trends:**  
   Putting price changes in context by focusing on Medicare users (primarily adults 65+).  
   We identify the **top five chronic conditions** affecting older adults and group drug pricing trends by the diseases they treat.

Our goal is to make Medicare drug pricing more transparent by identifying price hikes and visualizing how costs vary by manufacturer and by disease category.  
This website features both accessible dashboards and static visuals aimed at helping policymakers, researchers, and patients understand where spending is growing—and why.
